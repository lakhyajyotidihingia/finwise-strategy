# The Validation · FinWise

> Module 5 · Experimentation Methods. The experiment brief that tests the bet.

## Method

**Method:** A/B Test

**Why:** FinWise needs to determine whether automatically generating the first financial modeling output improves activation and trial-to-paid conversion. A standard A/B test provides a controlled 50/50 comparison between existing and simplified onboarding, isolating the impact of the change and delivering reliable causal evidence without the added complexity of multivariate testing.

_____

## Hypothesis + primary metric

If we automatically generate personalised financial modelling insights immediately after data import, then more trial users will reach their Aha moment and be more likely to convert to paid customers, because reducing manual steps helps users experience the product's core value faster.

**Primary metric:** First modelling output completion rate, target: Increase first modelling output completion by at least 10 %

_Experiment: Faster Time-to-Aha, Increase the percentage of trial users who successfully import financial data and generate their first personalised modelling output._

_Testing A simplified onboarding flow that automatically generates and displays a personalized financial modeling output immediately after data import, compared with the existing experience in a 50/50 A/B test. on New FinWise trial users, particularly small-business owners who have not yet generated their first financial modeling output.. Current experience: Users import financial data, then manually navigate to the modelling feature to generate their first financial insight, creating friction and delaying the Aha moment.._

_Predicted outcome: The simplified flow will increase the percentage of trial users reaching their first modelling output by at least 10 %, reducing time-to-value and potentially improving the current 2% trial-to-paid conversion rate.. If successful: Roll out the simplified onboarding to all new trial users and monitor whether improved activation translates into higher paid conversion and long-term retention.. If unsuccessful: Investigate whether friction lies in data import, modeling accuracy, or insight relevance. Use user feedback and funnel analysis to identify the barrier before testing a revised experience.._

_____

## Guardrail + read date

- **Guardrail:** Modeling errors and onboarding abandonment should not be increase by 5%
- **Read date:** 14 days after launch
