# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 3 · Optimization, FinWise's 2% trial-to-paid conversion rate shows that very few users experience enough value to justify paying, while its 40% one-year retention rate indicates difficulty sustaining that value after conversion. Together, these metrics suggest FinWise needs to optimize activation, monetization, and retention before scaling growth.

**Model:** Freemium, Freemium removes the risk of committing to a paid trial, potentially improving activation and conversion, but some users may remain on the free plan indefinitely. FinWise must balance delivering enough free value to demonstrate its benefits while reserving compelling ongoing value for paid subscribers.

_____

## Recommendation

ntroduce a freemium model with no credit card required for new small-business users, replacing the current paid-trial entry barrier. Allow users to import financial data and experience their first personalized modeling output for free, while reserving advanced forecasting and automated insights for paid tiers.

**The pricing bet:** If FinWise removes the upfront payment barrier and lets users experience their first financial modeling insight for free, more visitors will sign up, discover the product's value, and eventually convert to paid plans. Validate this through an A/B test, measuring visitor-to-signup conversion, first modeling output completion, and ultimately paid conversion and revenue per visitor.

_____

## Model signal

This directly targets our Module 4 leading indicator, Visitor-to-Trial Signup Conversion Rate, by reducing the perceived risk of trying FinWise. However, the data shows that higher signups and imports haven't translated into higher paid conversion, so the free experience must help users reach meaningful financial value before upgrading.
