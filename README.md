### Slide 9 · The Validation (Experiment)
- **Method:** **Method:** A/B Test**Why:** FinWise needs to determine whether automatically generating the first financial modeling output improves activation and trial-to-paid conversion. A standard A/B test provides a controlled 50/50 comparison between existing and simplified onboarding, isolating the impact of the change and delivering reliable causal evidence without the added complexity of multivariate testing.
- **Hypothesis + metric:** If we automatically generate personalised financial modelling insights immediately after data import, then more trial users will reach their Aha moment and be more likely to convert to paid customers, because reducing manual steps helps users experience the product's core value faster.

**Primary metric:** First modelling output completion rate, target: Increase first modelling output completion by at least 10 %

**The pricing bet:** If FinWise removes the upfront payment barrier and lets users experience their first financial modeling insight for free, more visitors will sign up, discover the product's value, and eventually convert to paid plans. ### Slide 11 · The Story (Insights)
- **Friction:** My biggest challenge was distinguishing surface-level metrics from the real problems affecting growth. I overcame this by analyzing user behavior, identifying patterns, and using data-driven hypotheses to design experiments that address root causes rather than symptoms.
- **Aha moment:** My biggest realization was that increased engagement doesn't necessarily translate into conversion or retention. True product growth comes from helping users experience meaningful value, not simply driving more signups, clicks, or feature usage.
- **Takeaways:** This course transformed how I approach product growth. I learned to connect user behavior to business outcomes, identify meaningful North Star metrics, select appropriate experimentation methods, and validate assumptions with data. Most importantly, I learned to experiment with purpose, prioritize user value, and make evidence-based product decisions rather than relying on intuition.
