## The data pattern

The headline metric is Trial-to-Paid Conversion stuck at around 2%, but the real pattern behind it is a potential failure to turn initial product engagement into meaningful value, because trial signups and financial data imports fluctuate or improve without corresponding conversion growth. This suggests users may not be reaching the personalised financial modelling insights that motivate them to pay.

_Metric patterns: Conversion, This looks like a Ceiling pattern because conversion remains flat at around 2% throughout the 13 months.. Modeling usage, This looks like a Cliff pattern because usage drops sharply from 58.06% to 36.04% in the final month, despite an overall volatile upward trend.. Import usage, This looks like a Ceiling pattern because usage recovers from 30.51% and then stabilizes around 40–45% in the final months.. Session duration, This looks like a Cliff pattern because duration drops sharply from 12.11 minutes in June to 5.14 minutes in August, before partially recovering.. Sessions/user, This looks like a Ceiling pattern because sessions recover from 3 to 9, then level off at 9 in the final two months, although more data is needed to confirm a sustained plateau.._

## Leading indicators

- **Visitor-to-trial signup conversion rate.:** Reducing perceived risk through a free trial with no credit card required and clearer value messaging encourages hesitant visitors to start exploring FinWise.
- **Percentage of trial users who successfully import financial data.:** Simplifying bank connections, offering CSV uploads, and reducing setup friction help more users reach their first personalized modeling output.

_____

## Lagging indicators

- **90-day paid customer retention rate.:** Sustained retention demonstrates that users find enough ongoing value in FinWise to continue paying beyond their initial trial.
