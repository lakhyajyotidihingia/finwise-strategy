Experiment · M5

How I test it

Method

Test design

**Method:** A/B Test**Why:** FinWise needs to determine whether automatically generating the first financial modeling output improves activation and trial-to-paid conversion. A standard A/B test provides a controlled 50/50 comparison between existing and simplified onboarding, isolating the impact of the change and delivering reliable causal evidence without the added complexity of multivariate testing.

Recommendation

The move

ntroduce a freemium model with no credit card required for new small-business users, replacing the current paid-trial entry barrier. Allow users to import financial data and experience their first personalized modeling output for free, while reserving advanced forecasting and automated insights for paid tiers.

**The pricing bet:** If FinWise removes the upfront payment barrier and lets users experience their first financial modeling insight for free, more visitors will sign up, discover the product's value, and eventually convert to paid plans. Validate this through an A/B test, measuring visitor-to-signup conversion, first modeling output completion, and ultimately paid conversion and revenue per visitor.

Reflection · Capstone

What I learned

Friction

The hard part

My biggest challenge was distinguishing surface-level metrics from the real problems affecting growth. I overcame this by analyzing user behavior, identifying patterns, and using data-driven hypotheses to design experiments that address root causes rather than symptoms.
Aha

The moment it clicked

My biggest realization was that increased engagement doesn't necessarily translate into conversion or retention. True product growth comes from helping users experience meaningful value, not simply driving more signups, clicks, or feature usage.
Takeaways

What stuck

This course transformed how I approach product growth. I learned to connect user behavior to business outcomes, identify meaningful North Star metrics, select appropriate experimentation methods, and validate assumptions with data. Most importantly, I learned to experiment with purpose, prioritize user value, and make evidence-based product decisions rather than relying on intuition.
